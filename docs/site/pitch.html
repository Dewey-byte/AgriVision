02 / 12 The problem

Banana disease spreads faster than farmers can inspect

  • Panama disease, Black Sigatoka & BBTV cause severe, spreading yield loss
  • Manual inspection is slow, labor-intensive, and error-prone at plantation scale
  • Delayed, inconsistent diagnosis lets hotspots grow unchecked

03 / 12 The gap

Existing AI systems stay on the ground

  • Most banana AI works on close-range leaf/fruit images, not aerial plantation views
  • Few systems offer heatmaps or geotagged disease mapping for targeted intervention
  • BBTV is underrepresented — most detect only one or two diseases
  • No integrated Philippine, aerial, three-disease monitoring tool exists
04 / 12 Our solution

Drone eyes + YOLOv8 + a map that shows where disease is

  • Capture aerial RGB frames from a drone/phone mirror
  • Detect Panama, Black Sigatoka & BBTV with YOLOv8
  • Map detections into heatmaps & geotagged pins
  • Report health summaries as JSON, CSV & interactive maps
